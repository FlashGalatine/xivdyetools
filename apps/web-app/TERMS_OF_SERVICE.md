XIV Dye Tools is a free, fan-made colour toolkit for Final Fantasy XIV. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or sponsored by Square Enix. By using the site you agree to what follows. If you do not
agree, please don't use it — nothing here costs you anything to walk away from.

## What the site does

Nine tools run in your browser: the Palette Extractor, Harmony Explorer, Comparison, Gradient
Builder, Dye Mixer, Accessibility checker, Budget finder, Swatch Matcher, and the community Presets
browser. Alongside them the site can show market-board prices, read a `.chara` file you drop in to
name the gear in a glamour, and let you save palettes and collections on your own device.

Most of this works without an account and without sending anything anywhere. The exceptions are
listed in [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md).

## Accounts

You never need an account to use the colour tools. You need one only to submit, edit or vote on
community presets.

- Signing in goes through Discord or XIVAuth. We never see or store a password.
- Signing in creates an account record holding your provider ID and username. Your username is
  shown as the author on presets you publish.
- Your session is a token held in your browser. Clearing your browser's site data signs you out.
- You are responsible for what happens under your account. If you think someone else has access to
  it, revoke the app's access with your provider and tell us.
- We may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms.

### What we ask of you

Don't submit anything offensive, harassing, sexually explicit, hateful, deceptive, advertising, or

anything unrelated to dyes.

### Moderation

Submissions go through automated content checks before they appear. A submission that clears them
is published straight away and logged for audit. One that gets flagged is held for a human
moderator instead, and so is one where a check runs but cannot reach a verdict — a service outage,
say. When in doubt, nothing goes live.

Moderators may remove or edit any submission, and repeated violations may cost you the ability to
submit. We can also remove content for reasons that have nothing to do with you — a legal request,
or a change to what the site hosts.

### Appeals and removal

If you think your content was moderated wrongly, or you want something you submitted taken down:

1. Join the Discord: <https://discord.gg/rzxDHNr6Wv>
2. Ask in **#dyetools-issues-and-suggestions**, or DM "Flash Galatine"
3. Include your Discord user ID and the name of the preset

Appeals are reviewed within 7 days. To have your account record and everything submitted under it
removed, see [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md).
