# Getting Started with the Web App

**Your first steps with XIV Dye Tools**

---

## Welcome to XIV Dye Tools!

The web app gives you 9 tools to explore FFXIV dye colors. Whether you're planning a glamour, designing housing, or just curious about color combinations, these tools have you covered.

There is no home screen to click through: the app opens straight into a tool — the **Color Harmony Explorer** by default, or whichever tool a shared link points at — and you switch between the nine from the bar at the top.

---

## The Interface

Everything hangs off one bar and one drawer:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ✦ XIV Dye Tools  [Harmony][○][○][○][○][○][○][○][○]  📜 ⓘ EN ☾ ⚙ │  ← console bar
├────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────┤
│  Options   │                                    │ Color Palette  │
│ (settings  │        the active tool             │ (dye picker    │
│  for this  │                                    │  drawer)       │
│  tool)     │                                    │                │
└────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────┘
```

### The console bar

- **Tool switcher.** On a desktop-sized window the nine tools sit in the bar as a row of small icon chips — the **tool rail**. The active tool's chip is filled in and shows its short name (**Harmony**, **Extractor**, **Vision**, **Compare**, **Gradient**, **Mixer**, **Presets**, **Budget**, **Swatch**); hover any other chip and its name unrolls. On a phone or narrow window the rail folds into a **title menu**: the bar shows the current tool's name with a small ▾, and tapping it opens a two-column list of all nine tools with a one-line description of each.
- **What's New** (the scroll icon) — the release history, newest release open, earlier ones collapsed. It also pops up on its own the first time you open the app after an update.
- **About XIV Dye Tools** (the ⓘ icon) — version, build date, dye count, links, and attribution.
- **Language** — a globe plus a two-letter code (**EN**, **JA**, **DE**, **FR**, **KO**, **ZH**); on phones the code alone. Opens the language picker.
- **Theme** — a sun or moon icon; opens the theme picker (see below).
- **Advanced Settings** (the gear) — a slide-over panel from the right (see below).

### Options — the settings column

On desktop a column on the left of the tool holds that tool's settings: things like the harmony type, step counts, matching algorithm, display options, dye filters and the Market Board toggle. It changes with the tool. On phones the same controls live inside the gear slide-over instead, so "the settings column (the gear icon on phones)" is how the tool guides refer to it.

### The Color Palette drawer

The **Color Palette** on the right is the dye picker for every tool that takes dyes (all but Palette Extractor and Community Presets). Click a swatch and it lands in the active tool — as the base dye in Harmony, an endpoint in Gradient, a slot in Mixer, and so on. Inside it you have:

- **Search dyes...** by name, and filter chips **All · Metallic · Pastel · Dark · Vibrant**.
- **Spectrum** chips — **Standard Spectrum**, **Wide Spectrum #1**, **Wide Spectrum #2**, **Unconsolidated** — for the Patch 7.5 dye groups. Budget Suggestions starts on Unconsolidated only, since consolidated dyes share a market listing.
- Colour groups (**Whites … Pinks**) with every dye as a swatch. Hover or tap a swatch to reveal a **★**; click it to add the dye to **Favorites** at the top of the drawer (up to 40 — see [Favorites & Collections](favorites-collections.md)).
- **Custom Color** — type a hex code (or use the little colour well) and press **Apply** to send an arbitrary colour into the tool instead of a dye. Shown in Harmony, Gradient, Mixer, Swatch, Accessibility, Comparison and Budget.
- A **dice** (pick a random dye) and a **broom** (clear the tool's dyes) in the drawer header.

On desktop the drawer is open from the start. On phones it starts **closed** and slides over the tool when you tap the round palette button near the bottom-right corner; the first time, a small callout — *"New here? Tap the palette button to pick dyes."* — points at that button until you open the drawer or dismiss it.

## Quick Tour of the Tools

In the order they appear in the rail (which is also the order of the `1`–`9` keys):

### 1. Color Harmony Explorer
**"Show me dyes that look good together"** — pick a base dye and get complementary, triadic, analogous, tetradic and other companions on a tappable colour wheel.

### 2. Palette Extractor
**"I have a picture, find me its dyes"** — drop in a screenshot, click any spot to sample it, or **Auto-extract** the dominant colours; every colour is matched to the closest dye.

### 3. Accessibility Checker
**"How will this look to colorblind players?"** — see up to four dyes through five vision lenses with a per-pair readout.

### 4. Dye Comparison
**"Put dyes side by side"** — a duel view for two to four dyes with a verdict on how alike they really are.

### 5. Gradient Builder
**"Create a gradient between two dyes"** — a ramp of 3–12 steps, each step matched to a real dye, with pins to bend the ramp through a dye you want.

### 6. Dye Mixer
**"Blend two dyes together"** — six blend models × five ratios, thirty real blends at once, each with its nearest dye.

### 7. Community Presets
**"Show me community creations"** — browse, save and submit palettes; the eight categories are jobs, grand companies, seasons, events, aesthetics, appearance, zones and raids & trials.

### 8. Budget Suggestions
**"Find affordable alternatives"** — a price ledger of look-alike dyes on your world, from Universalis.

### 9. Swatch Matcher
**"Match my character's colors"** — the character creator's colour sheets, or a `.chara` file, matched to dyes.

## Your First Color Match

Let's find dyes that go with one you already own:

1. Open the app — you are in the **Color Harmony Explorer**.
2. Open the **Color Palette** (on a phone, tap the palette button) and click a dye. It becomes the hub of the wheel.
3. Pick a harmony from the icon row above the wheel — **Complementary**, **Triadic**, and so on.
4. The cards under the wheel are the dyes for each slot, with a distance number showing how far each is from the ideal colour. Tap a small swatch dot on a card to swap in an alternate.
5. Press **Share** to copy a link that reopens the same dye and harmony for anyone.

Or start from a picture: switch to **Palette Extractor** (press `2`), drop in a screenshot, and click the pixel you care about.

## Saving Your Work

- **Favorites** — the **★** on any drawer swatch. Up to 40, shown at the top of the drawer.
- **Saved palettes** — several tools save what you built (**Save mix**, **Save swap**, **Save character colours**, **Make a palette**). They appear under **Community Presets → Saved**.
- **Share links** — every tool has a **Share** button (or press `Shift+S`) that copies a link carrying the exact dyes and settings.

Everything is stored in your browser on this device; nothing needs an account. See [Favorites & Collections](favorites-collections.md).

## Choosing a Theme

The app has a **Light** and a **Dark** theme, with Dark as the default.

1. Click the **sun / moon icon** in the console bar.
2. Pick **Light** or **Dark** — each card shows its background, surface and accent bands, and applies as soon as you click it. Press **Done** to close.

Your choice is saved automatically. `Shift+T` toggles between the two.

> The novelty themes (Forest, Ocean, Midnight, Moogle, and the rest) were retired in version 5.0. If you had one selected, you were moved to whichever of Light or Dark matches it.

## Language

Click the language code in the bar to choose **English, 日本語, Deutsch, Français, 한국어** or **中文**. Dye names, tool text and the curated preset descriptions all follow. `Shift+L` cycles to the next language.

## Advanced Settings (the gear)

The gear opens a slide-over with three collapsible cards:

- **Data** — *reset · clear saved dyes, favorites, palettes*: **Reset Settings**, **Clear Dyes**, **Clear Favorites**, **Clear Saved Palettes** (each asks you to confirm and cannot be undone) and **Reset Tutorial**.
- **Backup** — **Export** your settings as a JSON file, or **Import** one.
- **Behaviour** — **Performance Mode** (fewer animations for slower devices) and **Enable Analytics** (anonymous usage data, off by default).

On phones the current tool's settings sit above these cards, since there is no room for the settings column.

## First Visit

- A **Welcome to XIV Dye Tools** card introduces the four colour-in / colour-out tools and lists the other five. **Get started** drops you into the Harmony Explorer; **Take the tour** does the same and starts a short guided tour of it.
- The first time you open some tools you are offered a **Quick Tour** — *"Would you like a quick tour of this tool?"* — with **Start Tour**, **Skip** or **Disable All Tours**. **Reset Tutorial** in the gear brings the offers back.

## Tips for Best Results

### Color Matching
- FFXIV dyes don't cover all colors equally
- Some colors (like pink) have many options
- Others (like brown) have fewer but very specific matches

### Market Prices
- Some dyes are cheap (vendor)
- Others are expensive (rare drops, crafted)
- Turn on **Market Board** in the settings column and pick your data centre and world to see prices on result cards

### Colorblindness
- ~8% of men have some form of color vision deficiency
- Always check your glamour with the Accessibility tool
- Especially important for FC uniforms

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere (outside a text box) to see this list in the app.

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `1`–`9` | Switch tools: `1` Harmony, `2` Extractor, `3` Accessibility, `4` Comparison, `5` Gradient, `6` Presets, `7` Budget, `8` Swatch, `9` Mixer |
| `Shift+T` | Toggle theme |
| `Shift+L` | Cycle language |
| `Shift+S` | Share the current tool (same as its Share button) |
| `?` | Show the shortcuts panel |
| `Esc` | Close a modal, menu or dropdown |

Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a search box or hex field.
