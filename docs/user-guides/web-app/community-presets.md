# Community Presets

**Discover and share community dye palettes**

Community Presets is a gallery of dye palettes — the ones the community has posted, the fifteen official ones that ship with the app, the ones you have saved, and your own. Presets are 3 to 6 dyes with a name, a description, one or more categories, and optionally a link to the glamour and a preview picture. Every preset is keyed by the game's own dye numbers, so it opens the same in any language.

> **Note**: This tool was previously called "Preset Browser" in v3.x.

---

## Browsing Presets

### Tabs

Four tabs across the top, each with a live count:

| Tab | What it lists |
|-----|---------------|
| **Community** | Everything the community has posted (the default view) |
| **Official** | The palettes that ship with the app — Grand Companies, Seasons, Events — marked with an **Official** chip and the byline *"Ships with the app"* |
| **Saved** | Your saved shelf: presets you pressed **Save** on, plus palettes other tools saved to this device (see below) |
| **Mine** | Your own submissions — needs a sign-in |

If the presets service isn't answering, the Community tab shows a strip — **Community feed unavailable**: *"The presets service is not responding. The {n} official palettes are still here."* — and the Community count reads **—**. Official and Saved keep working; that is what they are for.

### Categories

Under the search row is the category rail, each button with a count: **All · Aesthetics · Jobs · Seasons · Events · Grand Companies · Appearance · Zones · Raids & Trials**. A preset has one primary category and up to two secondary ones, and it counts under all of them — so the numbers on the rail add up to more than the total, on purpose.

- **Appearance** - palettes matched to skin, hair and eyes
- **Zones** - palettes inspired by places
- **Raids & Trials** - duty-themed palettes

The old "Community" category is gone: community-ness is a tab now, not a shelf.

### Search and sort

One search field — **Search presets, dyes, tags** — matches names, descriptions, tags and dye names. Next to it a sort button cycles **Most Popular → Most Recent → Alphabetical**, and a count of **{n} presets** in the current view.

### Feed settings

The settings column (the gear icon on phones) has two sections for this tool:

- **Community** — **Show example images** (off makes every card a palette strip), **Blend Official into the feed** (mixes the curated palettes into Community; off keeps them in their own tab), **Hide palettes I cannot buy** (drops presets that need crafted or coffer-only dyes)
- **Saved** — **Show saved first** (pins your shelf to the top of every tab), **Keep deleted presets** (a saved preset the author removes stays, marked). *Stored locally — never sent to the server, works signed out.*

The **Account** section below them holds sign-in and **Submit Preset**.

## The Cards

Each card is a post: a picture-led top, then the name, two lines of description, a byline (**author · number of dyes · age**, e.g. *12d ago* or *built in*), and two buttons on the face:

- **Vote · {n}** / **Voted · {n}** — community presets only; one vote per preset per account, click again to take it back (there are no downvotes)
- **Save** / **Saved** — every preset; stores a snapshot on this device

The picture area shows the author's preview picture once a moderator has approved it; otherwise, if the preset has an example link, a striped band captioned **GLAMOUR SHOT · LINKED, NOT UPLOADED** with the link; otherwise the palette itself as a gradient. On the Saved tab, a preset whose author has since removed it carries the chip **Removed by its author — your saved copy**.

## Viewing a Preset

Click a card to open it. **Back to list** returns. The page shows:

- **Badges** — the primary and secondary categories, **Official** if it ships with the app, **★ {n} VOTES**
- The name, description and *"Created by {author}"*
- The **example link**, if any, at the top of the page
- **PALETTE** — a readable list: swatch, dye name, hex, source, and the vendor price or *"not sold"*
- **PALETTE COST** — a plain-language note: *"All 4 are vendor stock — buy the whole palette for 864 gil."* or *"2 of 5 are vendor stock. Jet Black · Pure White not sold — Venture Coffers."* Scrip, credit and coffer dyes are named with their source, never converted to gil.
- **Tags**
- **TAKE THIS PALETTE INTO** — four handoff buttons so the page doesn't dead-end: **Color Harmony Explorer** (*Build around one of these*), **Dye Comparison** (*Measure them against each other* — the first four dyes), **Gradient Builder** (*Blend two of them* — the first two), **Accessibility Checker** (*Check they stay distinct* — the first four)
- Actions: **Copy Link**, **Vote · {n}** (community presets), and **Edit** / **Delete** on your own

## Saving

**Save** on any card keeps a copy on this device — up to 200, no sign-in needed. Saved copies survive signing out, the presets service being down, and the author deleting the original (kept and marked, unless you turn off **Keep deleted presets**). They appear on the **Saved** tab, together with the palettes the Dye Mixer and Swatch Matcher save to this device (see [Favorites & Collections](favorites-collections.md)).

## Signing In

Browsing and saving need no account. Voting and submitting do. Press **Vote** signed out and the sign-in sheet explains itself — *"Browsing and saving work without an account."* — with a **WHAT AN ACCOUNT ADDS** table (Browse and search ✓, Save presets ✓, Vote ★, Submit and edit ★) and two providers: **Discord** (*Most players already have one*) and **XIVAuth** (*Verifies your character*). *We store your display name and provider ID. No character data, no email, nothing sold.*

The **Account** section of the settings column offers the same two buttons (**Login with Discord** / **Login with XIVAuth**) and, once signed in, your name, **+ Submit Preset** and **Logout**.

## Submitting Presets

Press **+ Submit Preset** in the settings column (or **Make a palette → Submit to Community** in the Swatch Matcher, which arrives with the dyes filled in). The sheet is titled **Submit a preset** — *Reviewed before it appears* — and starts with a **HOW IT WILL LOOK** preview band, marked **DRAFT**, that redraws as you type.

| Field | Rules |
|-------|-------|
| **Name** — REQUIRED | 2–50 characters. *"Shown on the card. Not translated — write it in your own language."* |
| **Description** — REQUIRED | 10–200 characters. *"Two lines on the card, all of it on the page."* |
| **Category** — REQUIRED | *"First pick is the primary category. Up to 2 more, optional."* The first one you tap is marked **PRIMARY**; a fourth pick warns *"Up to 3 categories — remove one first"* |
| **Dyes** — 3–6 REQUIRED | Search by name and tap swatches in the grid; tap a chip to remove it. Every slot must be a real dye — no hex field here, because a preset has to be buyable |
| **Tags** — OPTIONAL | Comma-separated. *"Searchable. Job abbreviations work: mch, drk, whm."* |
| **Example link** — NEW · OPTIONAL | *"A glamour page on an allowed site — Eorzea Collection, the Lodestone, or your post on X, Bluesky, pixiv and a few others. We store the link, never a copy of the image."* Also accepted: Mirapri, Reddit, Instagram, Misskey. Checked when you leave the field |
| **Preview image (optional)** | *"PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB. Shown on your card once a moderator approves it."* |

Then **Cancel** or **Submit Preset**.

### What happens next

*"Submissions are checked before they go live: no duplicates of an existing palette, no names that break the code of conduct, and any example link must point to an allowed site. Expect a day."* In practice:

- A submission that passes the automatic content check goes live at once (*"Preset submitted successfully!"*); one that trips it waits for a moderator (*"Preset submitted! It will appear after moderator review."*)
- A preview picture always waits for a moderator, whatever the text did — until then the card shows the palette or the example link
- The same dye combination as an existing preset is refused: *"This dye combination already exists as "{name}""* — and if you're signed in, your vote is added to that one instead
- 10 submissions per day per account

## My Submissions

On the **Mine** tab (sign-in required) a **My Submissions** button opens your ledger: **PUBLISHED / TOTAL VOTES / AWAITING REVIEW** counts, then one row per submission with its status chip — **LIVE**, **IN REVIEW** (*"Queued for moderation. Most submissions clear within a day."*) or **NOT PUBLISHED**, with the moderator's actual rejection reason — and per-status actions: **View**, **Edit**, **Resubmit**, **Delete**.

### Editing

**Edit** (from the detail page or My Submissions) reopens the same fields for a preset you own: name, description, categories (primary and secondary), dyes (still 3–6), tags, example link, and the preview picture — choose a new file to replace it, or press **Remove** (*"Image under review"* while a new one waits). Only what you changed is sent; if nothing did, you'll see *"No changes to save"*. An edit that trips the content check goes back to review: *"Changes saved! Your edit is pending moderator review."*

**Delete** asks first — *"Are you sure you want to delete this preset? This action cannot be undone."*

## Tips

- **Descriptive names** help others find presets — the search reads dye names too, so name the palette, not the dyes
- **Tags with job abbreviations** (mch, drk, whm) are what people type
- **Link the glamour** — a card with an example link or picture gets looked at
- **Save before you leave** — the Saved tab is the one shelf that never goes offline
- **Vote honestly** to help the good ones rise
