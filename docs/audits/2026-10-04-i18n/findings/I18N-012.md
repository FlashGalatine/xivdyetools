# I18N-012: The character-file card joins two ja / zh sentences with an ASCII space after 。
**Tier:** P3 · **Locale(s):** ja zh · **Deploy unit:** apps/web-app · **Generated?** no · **Origin:** MAIN

## Location
- `apps/web-app/src/components/chara-file-card.ts:158` — `${this.privacyHint()} ${note}`

## Evidence
- Renders "…この端末にとどまります。 編集した入手方法は…". Korean writes a space between sentences, so ko is unaffected. The join predates #223, which only swapped the hint.

## Fix
- Join with a locale-aware separator (none for ja / zh), or move the join into one localized key.
