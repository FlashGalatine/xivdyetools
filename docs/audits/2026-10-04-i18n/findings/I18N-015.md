# I18N-015: Glamour card footer wraps at any space: counts split from their nouns, and long German is cut off
**Tier:** P3 · **Locale(s):** all six (truncation in de) · **Deploy unit:** packages/svg · **Generated?** no · **Origin:** MAIN

## Location
- `packages/svg/src/glamour-card.ts:113` `wrapFoot`

## Evidence
- Measured with `estimateTextWidth`: de with both clauses gives "5 von 12 gefärbten Teilen · 3" / "durch Zwilling benannt · 2 ohne L…"; en splits "… · 3 named from" / "a twin · …". The embed carries the full list, so nothing is lost outright.

## Fix
- Break only at " · " clause boundaries (or keep each count with its noun), and allow a third line or shorten the de clauses.
