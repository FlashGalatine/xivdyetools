# I18N-002: og-worker localizes the card but every outbound URL it emits — including its meta-refresh — drops `?lang=`
**Tier:** P2 · **Locale(s):** ja de fr ko zh · **Deploy unit:** og-worker · **Generated?** no
Continues `2026-08-20-og-worker-i18n/OG-I18N-001`, which fixed the *inbound* share links; the worker's own *outbound* links were not covered.

## Location
- `apps/og-worker/src/og-data-generator.ts:76` — `withLang()` exists and is applied to **every** `imageUrl` (lines 125, 200, 215, 247, 277, 290, 340, 388, 426, 456, 496).
- The same file's 15 `url:` sites (124, 199, 214, 246, 276, 289, 345, 387, 425, 455, 495, 520, 536, 548, 828) are built **without** `withLang()`.
- `apps/og-worker/src/og-data-generator.ts:625` — `<meta http-equiv="refresh" content="0;url=${ogData.url}">`; `:606` `og:url`; `:616` `twitter:url`; `:676` the visible "open" link.

## Evidence
- `grep -n "withLang" og-data-generator.ts` returns 12 hits, all `imageUrl`; `grep -n "url:"` returns 15 hits, none wrapped.
- Line 625 is navigational, not advisory: a human who opens the share page is redirected to the app with the sharer's locale stripped, so a `?lang=ja` share shows a Japanese card and then lands the reader on a page in *their* default language.
- No test asserts that `ogData.url` carries a locale.

## Fix
- Wrap the `url:` sites in `withLang()` exactly as the `imageUrl` sites are — at minimum the meta-refresh target (625) and the body link (676), which are the two a person actually follows.
- Decide `og:url` separately: it is a canonicalisation hint, so carrying `?lang=` there is a deliberate SEO trade-off, not an obvious win.
