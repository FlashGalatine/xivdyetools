# BUG-015: A language switch empties settled Swatch matches and disables Share
**Severity:** MEDIUM · **Type:** State · **Deploy unit:** apps/web-app · **Covered by test?:** No regression assertion; see evidence.

## Location
- `apps/web-app/src/components/swatch-tool.ts:461`
- `apps/web-app/src/components/swatch-tool.ts:1314`
- `apps/web-app/src/components/swatch-tool.ts:1321`

## Evidence
- The locale subscriber calls inherited update(), which renders a fresh matchResultsContainer and a fresh disabled share button. renderRightPanel ends by updating selection card, empty state, grid and handoff only. updateColorGrid never calls updateMatchResults or updateShareButton, and Swatch has no onUpdate override. selectedColor and matchedDyes survive but their cards do not. A pending price completion can accidentally repopulate cards, so use a settled selection with prices off.
- Reproduce: Pick a swatch cell with market prices off and observe match cards plus enabled Share. Switch language. The selected cell/selection sentence remain, but cards are gone and Share is disabled. Pick a cell again to restore them.
- Test gap: swatch-tool.test.ts:1162-1169 rerenders only a loaded character card; :1324-1335 checks slot naming; :2027-2035 checks listener cleanup after update. None asserts forward matches and share state survive update. No suite was run by this reviewer. See [review and verification evidence](../evidence/).

## Fix
- Restore the derived match cards and share parameters during/after every render without reloading the selected cell or duplicating subscriptions; add a settled forward-match locale regression.
