## Evidence
- Reproduction: Two moderators (or a double click) press Approve. Click 1 commits and edits to 'Approved by X'. Click 2 gets a 409, re-fetches and edits, so the message shows a plain 'now approved' review embed with no 'Approved by'.

The win's attribution field is dropped, and this edit lands after an extra GET round trip, so usually last.
  - Checked: review-message.ts:188-200 (file is 219 lines; cited :305 does not exist); presets-api/src/handlers/moderation.ts:234-236; preset-moderation.ts:242-245

## Fix
- When actionsFor() returns [], keep the original embed and append a 'Review' field (or skip the edit). Replace the embed only when buttons are being re-issued.
