# BUG-146: gradient.ts ROW_WIDTHS.lead=28 ellipsises two-digit merged step ranges ('9–10' -> '9–…', '10–12' -> '10…')
**Severity:** LOW · **Type:** Edge case · **Deploy unit:** packages/svg · **Covered by test?** no · **Origin:** MAIN · **Other units:** packages/bot-logic (range text), apps/discord-worker (consumer)

## Location
- `packages/svg/src/gradient.ts:103`

## Evidence
- Reproduction: /gradient steps:12 where adjacent steps 9-10 (or 10-12) resolve to one dye -> row lead prints '9–…' / '10…'
- The lead is drawn via frame.ts:570 fitText(lead, 28, 13, 'mono'), which is 8.06 px per char (base.ts:310, factor 0.62). bot-logic gradient.ts:310 builds `${start}–${end}` and steps go up to 12 (schemas.ts:400). '9–10' is 32.2 px, so it renders as '9–…'; '10–12' is 40.3 px, so it renders as '10…'. The step span is lost from the card.
  - Checked: packages/svg/src/gradient.ts:103; packages/svg/src/frame.ts:185-202,570; packages/bot-logic/src/commands/gradient.ts:310; fixtures only use single-digit ranges: gradient.test.ts:23, frame-budget.test.ts:83
- Second, independent check (severity LOW): The defect is real and happens as described, but it is a cosmetic truncated label, not a wrong result. The steps option allows up to 12 (apps/discord-worker/src/commands/schemas.ts:399-400). capGradientRows (packages/bot-logic/src/commands/gradient.ts:106-116) merges adjacent steps that land on the same dye, and :310 turns a merged range into the text `${start}–${end}`. That text is the row lead (packages/svg/src/gradient.ts:173), which gets 28 px (:103). frame.ts:570 draws it with fitText(lead, 28, 13, 'mono'), and at 13 px mono each character is counted as 8.06 px (frame.ts:181, base.ts estimateTextWidth; the en dash is not counted as wide). So '9–10' measures 32.2 px and becomes '9–…', and '10–12' measures 40.3 px and becomes '10…'. A single '10' to '12' measures 24.2 px and fits. No test uses a range with two digits. I put it at LOW rather than MEDIUM because nothing shown is wrong: the dye name, the ΔE and the swatch pair on the row are still correct, and the strip at the top still shows every step. The ellipsis shows the label was cut rather than giving a false value. It also needs 10 or more steps plus a merge in the tail of the gradient. Under the brief's scale that is cosmetic, so LOW.
- Origin: git show 8ecb878f:packages/svg/src/gradient.ts has `lead: 28` at :103; git diff --stat 8ecb878f HEAD -- packages/svg packages/bot-logic/src/commands/gradient.ts is empty

## Fix
- Widen ROW_WIDTHS.lead to ~44 and take the 16 px from `name`, keeping the sum at 368. Alternatively draw the range at CARD_TYPE.label 11 px.
Add a 12-step '10–12' fixture to frame-budget.test.ts.
Needs an svg publish, then consumer deploys (discord-worker).
