# BUG-083: chara-sheet.ts slot-card click re-renders the whole sheet and drops keyboard focus; selected state has no aria-pressed
**Severity:** LOW · **Type:** State · **Deploy unit:** apps/web-app · **Covered by test?** no · **Origin:** MAIN · **Other units:** none

## Location
- `apps/web-app/src/components/chara-sheet.ts:415`

## Evidence
- Reproduction: Tab to a THIS CHARACTER slot card and press Enter: the card is rebuilt, focus moves to body, and the next Tab restarts at the top of the page
- The click handler at chara-sheet.ts:415-418 calls render(), which runs clearContainer(this.container) at :212 and rebuilds every <button> card. The focused button is detached, so keyboard focus falls to body. Nothing restores focus (pickCharaSlot has no focus call), and cards carry no aria-pressed.
  - Checked: chara-sheet.ts:211-219 (clearContainer + rebuild), :295-300 (button card), :415-419; grep finds no focus or aria-pressed in the file
- Origin: git show 8ecb878f:apps/web-app/src/components/chara-sheet.ts:417 already calls this.render() in the click listener

## Fix
- Toggle the selection styles and aria-pressed on the old and new card in place instead of calling render(), or re-focus the rebuilt card for slot.slot after render.
