## Location
- `docs/user-guides/web-app/faq.md:99-104` — "expand the **Favorites** panel above the dye list, and press **Manage Collections**. That window has **Export All** and **Import**"
- `docs/user-guides/web-app/favorites-collections.md:87` — "expand the **Favorites** panel at the top of the Color Palette drawer and press **Manage Collections**"

## Evidence
- `apps/web-app/src/components/dye-selector.ts:556-565` — the only external caller of showCollectionManagerModal() is the DyeSelector's #manage-collections-btn (git grep finds no other caller outside the modal itself)
- `apps/web-app/src/components/v4-layout.ts:595-660` — harmony/accessibility/comparison/gradient/mixer all get leftPanel === rightPanel === mainPanel and `drawerContent: null`; mixer-tool.ts:627 skips renderLeftPanel in V4, and harmony/comparison/accessibility build the selector and then clearContainer(right) on the same element (harmony-tool.ts:1124, comparison-tool.ts:890, accessibility-tool.ts:745)

## Fix
- Remove the export/import steps, or say that saved palettes cannot be exported in this release (the Backup card exports settings only), until a route to the collection manager returns. Whether the orphaned modal is a product bug is the maintainer's call.
