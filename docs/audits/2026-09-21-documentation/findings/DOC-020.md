# DOC-020: the glossary defines "Special Dye" by an acquisition tier the schema does not have
**Severity:** MEDIUM · **Deploy unit:** repo-wide · **Baseline:** working tree = `main` `59572c0` + this branch's three spelling commits

## Location
- `docs/reference/glossary.md:94-95` — "Special Dye — Rare dyes obtained from crafting, dungeons, or events. Often more expensive."

## Evidence
- `packages/core/src/config/dye-vocabulary.ts:34-39` — `DYE_ACQUISITIONS` is exactly `Dye Vendor`, `The Firmament`, `Cosmic Exploration`, `Venture Coffers`. "Crafting", "dungeons" and "events" are not values in the schema at all.
- The 27 `category: "Special"` rows in `dyes.json` come from Venture Coffers (20), Cosmic Exploration (4) and The Firmament (3).
- `docs/maintainer/adding-dyes.md:279` gives the real meaning: "`Special` | Metallic, pastel, dark series" — a **colour family**, not a rarity or acquisition tier. The glossary and the maintainer guide contradict each other, and the glossary is the dictionary.

## Fix
- "Special Dye — the metallic, pastel and dark series (`category: "Special"`, 27 dyes). A colour family, not an acquisition tier; these come from Venture Coffers, Cosmic Exploration and The Firmament."
