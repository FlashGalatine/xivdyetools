# Discord Alternatives: October 2026 Refresh

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** Current. This refreshes the 2026-02-14 set in this folder: [README](./README.md), [comparison matrix](./05-comparison-matrix.md), [Telegram](./01-telegram.md), [Stoat](./02-stoat.md) (with 02a–02e), [Matrix](./03-matrix.md), [Rocket.Chat](./04-rocketchat.md), [shared libraries](./06-shared-libraries.md) and [bot-logic](./06d-bot-logic.md). Their bodies stay as written; their Status lines and links may change (see [the research index](../index.md)). Where they disagree with this one, this one wins.
**Method:** Six research reports, each followed by an adversarial fact-check. Claims the fact-checks corrected appear here in corrected form. Anything still unverified is marked *(unverified)*. Web search hit its 200-call cap during the research, so a few items rest on one source and are labeled as such.

In February, Discord said that from March every account would get a teen experience unless the user proved their age with a face scan or an ID. Discord delayed that plan on 2026-02-24. On 2026-09-22 it announced a different system: a model estimates each account's age group from account signals, and Discord says more than 90% of users will not be asked to do anything. The rollout was staged over the week from 2026-09-23. **Our bot keeps working unchanged.** None of its commands are age-restricted. What changed is that some users distrust Discord. Since February, Stoat has reached 1M registered accounts, but its bot API has not moved. Telegram added ephemeral messages. New candidates have appeared, mainly Fluxer. Every hosted chat platform we examined also has age rules of its own. This refresh also covers Japanese, Korean and Chinese messaging apps and non-chat surfaces, and it re-measures how much bot code is actually shared.

## 1. Bottom line

Ranked:

1. **Stay on Discord. The bot needs no change.**
   - None of our 18 commands is age-restricted, and the bot uses HTTP interactions only.
   - Discord's developer changelog says that "for most developers, nothing changes" [4][7].
   - The risk is user trust and reach, not breakage.
2. **Ask the community before building anything.**
   - Across the chat alternatives, the only FFXIV communities our capped search found were two tiny Stoat servers (53 and 6 members) and an NGA server on KOOK.
   - A one-week poll costs nothing (§8, step 2).
3. **Promote the web app as the fallback for people who leave Discord.**
   - Its tools need no account, so no platform age check sits in front of them. Whether publicly listed community presets bring the site itself under the UK OSA or similar laws was not researched.
   - It is already localized in six languages and mostly built.
   - Preset submission already works without Discord, through XIVAuth sign-in.
   - The remaining gaps: `navigator.share`, the `share_target` route, and og-worker's crawler list (per-tool share cards reach only the crawlers in `apps/og-worker/src/crawler-detector.ts`; Bluesky, Mastodon, Matrix, Stoat, Fluxer, LINE and KakaoTalk get the generic site card).
4. **If the poll names a platform, Telegram is the best-fitting second bot.**
   - Its webhooks fit Cloudflare Workers.
   - Bot API 10.1–10.3 added rich messages, ephemeral group messages and disabled buttons.
   - Estimate: about 4.5–6 dev-weeks for the core commands, including one-time shared work.
   - Caveats: we found no FFXIV evidence on Telegram, and it has its own 18+ rule in the EU, UK and Australia.
5. **Keep Stoat parked, or archive it.**
   - The park decision's own revisit trigger ("Discord privacy concerns") has fired, but nothing else has changed. Stoat still has no slash commands, buttons, ephemeral replies or HTTP delivery, and a bot there still needs a host that holds a persistent WebSocket.
   - FFXIV presence is almost zero.
   - Re-check against the three triggers in §4.
6. **Watch the other regional and social platforms, but don't build for them.**
   - LINE is the only plausible regional adapter (Japan), but Japanese and Korean players are visibly on Discord.
   - Chinese platforms need mainland real-name verification and mainland hosting.
   - A Bluesky reply bot is the cheapest bot to build (3.5–5 weeks), but every reply is public and it cannot take file input. Similarweb estimates its web audience fell 27% year on year [76].
7. **Fluxer: watch only.**
   - It has the same persistent-gateway hosting problem as Stoat, and slash commands and buttons are "planned" with no date [53].
   - Re-estimate if interactions ship. If they arrive as Discord-compatible HTTP interactions, much of discord-worker might carry over *(unverified)*.
8. **Offer the public API to community bot builders.**
   - `api-worker` already serves data.xivdyetools.app, with developer docs on developers.xivdyetools.app. Community developers on Telegram, Matrix or Fluxer could build their own bot on it, at no adapter cost to us.
   - Effort S: a "build a bot on our API" docs page. Check first whether it returns rendered cards or JSON only, and whether the 60 requests/min per-IP limit (`apps/api-worker/src/middleware/rate-limit.ts`) suits a hosted bot.

### What it means for our users

- **Adults classified as adult:** no change.
- **Teen and unconfirmed accounts can still use every command we have**, because none is age-restricted. New or low-activity accounts, such as new FFXIV players on fresh accounts, are the most likely to land in "unconfirmed". That doesn't affect our bot.
- **Adults the model misclassifies** lose access to age-restricted spaces until they confirm (User Settings > Account Status). Confirming means sharing a card, an ID or a face with a vendor, and after the 2025 breach that is the core of the distrust.
- **Whether the sensitive-media filter could blur our rendered PNGs** is unverified. It seems unlikely, because the filter targets content flagged as sensitive.
- **The indirect risk** is fewer active users in the communities where the bot is installed.

### What it means for the bot

- **No code change is needed.**
  - PR #8622 lists four runtime behaviors and none of them needs action: custom-status visibility, age-restricted Activities, parent-set spending limits, and more DMs filtered into message requests [7].
  - The PR is hedged with "for most developers".
- **Bots cannot read a user's age group.** The User object at docs HEAD has no age field [7]. Absence from the docs is not proof.
- **Repo check:**
  - No `nsfw`, `age_restricted` or `ageRestricted` anywhere in `apps/` or `packages/`.
  - `apps/discord-worker/src/index.ts:4` handles interactions "via HTTP instead of the Gateway WebSocket".
  - There are no Activities and no Social SDK.
- **Age-restricted commands are not new:** the docs date from 2022. Apps listed in the App Directory "cannot contain any age-restricted commands" [8]. Keep it that way.

### Community sentiment (kept separate from the facts above)

- **Facts about sentiment**
  - Discord's CTO acknowledged strong skepticism [9].
  - Bugs were reported in the first days [23].
  - A Hacker News thread (123 points and 88 comments when fetched) focused on three things: trust that data is really deleted, whether unverified accounts can be identified, and whether the law actually compels this. It named Matrix, Jami, IRC and Fluxer as alternatives [27].
- **Opinion**
  - EFF: platforms should not adopt verification that the law does not require [24].
  - MakeUseOf: a wrong guess still forces an adult to hand over more data [26].
  - PCWorld: "less terrible than I expected", though still wary [25].
- **Anecdote only (unverified):** mass Nitro cancellations, spikes in search interest, a "500M accounts" figure, and a self-selected sample of 620 app reviews.

## 4. Stoat, revisited

### Why it was parked

- **The decision (2026-07-30).** Recorded in [05-package-and-app-audit.md](../monorepo-2.0/05-package-and-app-audit.md) §6 item 12: "there is currently no user demand for a Revolt bot. Revisit if/when Discord poses more significant privacy concerns."
- **Where the trigger is repeated.** The trigger appears again in [monorepo-2.0/README.md](../monorepo-2.0/README.md) line 29. `docs/architecture/overview.md:376` says only "no current demand".
- **What is in the repo.** `apps/stoat-worker` has 4 of the 18 commands: `ping`, `help`, `about` and `dye info`.
  - Commands use prefixes (`!xd`, `!xivdye`), and replies are text embeds only.
  - It runs on `revolt.js` 7.2.0.
  - It has no image cards and no storage, and locale is hard-coded to `en`.
  - The reaction listener is not wired (BUG-038).
  - There is no host and no deploy workflow.
  - Sprint 12 security fixes (0.3.2) are in progress.

**Hosting**
- **Node host.** A Fly.io instance or a VPS is simplest. The February price of about $3–5/month has not been re-checked.
- **Durable Object relay: why it needs a watchdog.** An outbound WebSocket keeps a DO alive for at most **15 minutes**, and frames arriving on a client socket do not count as activity. The DO also restarts on every deploy. So an **alarm watchdog is mandatory**: either wake about every 60 s (≈1,440 wake-ups a day), or reconnect and resume within 15 minutes [90].
- **Durable Object relay: cost.** One DO connected 24/7 uses 10,800 GB-s/day against the Free plan's 13,000, about 17% headroom. A beta DO plus a production DO (21,600) exceeds it [89]. The account's actual plan is unverified, and repo sources disagree (`apps/discord-worker/scripts/check-bundle-size.mjs` calls 3,072 KiB the paid-plan limit); §8 step 5 settles it.
- **Durable Object relay: library fit.** `stoat.js` uses the global `WebSocket`, `fetch` and `setInterval` and imports no Node built-ins. It may therefore not need `nodejs_compat`, which discord-worker deliberately removed (ARCH-001). That is *unverified*, and settling it takes a short spike.

### Policy documents: 12 files per platform

**What a new platform needs.** A privacy policy and a Terms of Service, each in six languages, modeled on `apps/discord-worker/PRIVACY_POLICY*.md` and `TERMS_OF_SERVICE*.md`.

**The rules** (`.agents/skills/audit-shared/policy-documents.md`):
- All six variants change in one commit and carry the same date.
- Every translation carries an "English prevails" notice.
- A reviewer who reads each language checks its meaning. In the 2026-09-20 pass, reviewers found 32 corrections after the mechanical gate had already passed.

**Tooling updates.** `policy-locale-parity.py` hard-codes its document list. That list, the table in `policy-documents.md` and the security-audit Step 0 reading list all need updating.

**The cheaper alternative** is to amend the Discord documents to cover every platform. The downside is that every platform then shares one "Last Updated" date and the Discord-specific wording, such as deletion requests by "Discord User ID".

### Data promises

**Stored platform IDs.** Any platform ID we store for preferences or rate limits must be listed in the policy and given a deletion path. Telegram also requires deletion on request and when the bot shuts down [32].
Its §4.4 adds two more: user data must be encrypted at rest and stored separately from its
encryption key, and users must be alerted to any breach [32]. Before building, check whether the
platform's own at-rest encryption for KV and D1 meets the first; if it does not, budget
application-level encryption and key management.

**Analytics Engine.** Rows there cannot be deleted per user. discord-worker writes the raw Discord ID to `blob2` today (`apps/discord-worker/src/services/analytics.ts`), and the bot policy discloses it. Telegram's deletion-on-request term [32] rules that out for a Telegram adapter: write no ID, or an HMAC with a daily-rotated salt. Decide whether Discord moves to the same scheme; that would be a six-language policy change.

**Rules that carry over to every platform:**
- The never-record list on `CommandEvent` (option values, hex, search text, guild or channel IDs, error messages).
- Persistent logs off (FINDING-022), on Node hosts too.

**LINE adds stored images.** Hosting generated PNGs on our public URL is new storage that must be disclosed.

### Presets and moderation

- **Preset identity sits in Discord-named columns, but the web app already accepts XIVAuth sign-in.** XIVAuth-only accounts store their oauth UUID in `author_discord_id`, so a namespaced identity has a precedent. Only the bot path (`X-User-Discord-ID`, `MODERATOR_IDS`, `createBotSignatureV2`) is Discord-only.
- **Any new `tg:`-style namespace inherits the open B1 ban-evasion gap** (`apps/presets-api/src/middleware/ban-check.ts`, documented 2026-09-16). Close B1 before adding identities.
- **Moderation runs through Discord.** Notifications go through the `DISCORD_WORKER` binding and moderation-worker buttons.
- **Shared secrets.** All bots use one `BOT_API_SECRET` and one `BOT_SIGNING_SECRET`, so each new bot widens the blast radius. Use per-bot credentials.
- **Perspective API sunsets 2026-12-31** (`DEPRECATIONS.md`, Perspective entry). Flagged content then lands on human moderators.
- **Recommendation: on any new platform, presets are read-only.** Browsing needs none of the above.

### Maintenance per new app

- **Pipeline:** CI jobs, a deploy workflow, and a `wrangler.toml` with `[observability] enabled = false` plus the invariants check.
- **Rate limits and secrets:** `RL_*` namespace IDs, and entries in `docs/operations/SECRET_ROTATION.md` and `DEPLOY_ENVIRONMENTS.md`.
- **Docs:** version-table rows and a `CLAUDE.md`.
- **New bot strings** have to pass the six-language locale-quality gate.
- **Node apps are invisible to the dead-code gate,** because `listTracked()` excludes them, as it does stoat-worker today. A `bot-logic` export that only a Node app uses then looks dead.
- **Account setup takes lead time:** a LINE Official Account, a Matrix homeserver, or a Bluesky account labeled `bot`.
- **Ongoing time per app: not estimated.** The parked Stoat app's security-sprint cost shows the floor is above zero. Before §8 step 7, compare the 4.5–6-week Telegram figure with the open remediation backlog.
